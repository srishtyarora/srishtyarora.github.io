01 / About

Rigorous research.
Real public health impact.

My work brings together applied epidemiology and academic research. I earned my PhD in Health Behavior and Health Education at The University of Texas at Austin, following an MPH and training in dental sciences.

My research examines tobacco, nicotine and cannabis use, mental health, social and digital contexts, and health disparities. Before my doctoral research, I worked in infectious disease surveillance, immunization, and emergency public health response at the Texas Department of State Health Services.

I am interested in applying strong quantitative methods to public health, population health, health outcomes research, program evaluation, and related research roles.
