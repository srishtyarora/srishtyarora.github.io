01 / About

Research grounded in evidence and public health practice

I am an epidemiologist and quantitative public health researcher with a PhD in Health Behavior and Health Education from The University of Texas at Austin, a Master of Public Health in Epidemiology, and a bachelor’s degree in dental sciences.

My research examines tobacco, nicotine and cannabis use, mental health, social and digital contexts, and health disparities. Before my doctoral research, I worked in infectious disease surveillance, immunization, and emergency public health response at the Texas Department of State Health Services.

I am interested in applying strong quantitative methods to public health, population health, health outcomes research, program evaluation, and related research roles.

03 / Research

Selected research themes

Depressive symptoms & cannabis use

Examining longitudinal relationships between mental health and cannabis initiation, smoking, and vaping among young adults.

Digital & social environments

Investigating how social media exposure, peer contexts, and family communication relate to health behaviors.

Longitudinal cohort methods
